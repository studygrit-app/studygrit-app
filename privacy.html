STUDYGRIT - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to the StudyGrit Android app (com.studygrit.app)

Data Collection

StudyGrit collects and stores your study sessions, plans, tasks, and preferences locally on your device using SQLite. When you sign in with Google, your data is synced to Supabase cloud servers to enable cross-device access.

Authentication

We use Google Sign-In via Supabase for authentication. We only access your basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) to identify your account. We do not access your Google Drive, contacts, or other data.

Cloud Storage

If you choose to sign in, your study data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Each user can only access their own data via Row Level Security policies.

Local Storage

If you use the app without signing in, all data stays on your device only. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data.

Notifications

StudyGrit uses local notifications for reminders and motivational messages. These are processed entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Crash Reporting

We use anonymous crash reporting (Sentry) to improve app stability. No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data by: (1) using Reset All Data in Settings, (2) signing out and uninstalling the app, or (3) contacting us to request cloud data deletion.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Supabase (cloud database)
  • Sentry (crash reporting)

Each service has its own privacy policy.

Contact
