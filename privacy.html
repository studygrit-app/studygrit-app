STUDYGRIT - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Applies to Lifefile, the StudyGrit Android app on Google Play (com.studygrit.app), the StudyGrit website, and the StudyGrit Voice apps (Android com.studygrit.voice and Windows)

What We Store

StudyGrit stores what you put into it: your tasks, projects and goals, the blocks on your calendar, your reminders and planning settings, and your chat with the StudyGrit AI (your messages, its replies and the files or screenshots you attach). It also stores your "brain": the notes you write or share into the app (including links you paste), the quiz cards made from them and your review history, the files you attach (PDFs, images), your shelf names and settings, and the birthdays you add (a name and a date, optionally the year and a note) so the app can remind you. The messages and screenshots you send to your inbox are stored privately to build your notes. On the phone this is kept in your private cloud account, so the Android app and the website show the same data. A new account starts empty: nobody else's data is ever shown to it.

The AI Chat

When you send a message to the StudyGrit AI (in the chat, the "Tell StudyGrit" box, or "Ask about this card"), our server sends your message and the parts of YOUR OWN data the AI needs to answer (for example today's tasks and calendar, or the note you asked about) over an encrypted connection to our AI service provider, which writes the reply. The provider processes this only as our service provider to produce the reply; it is not used for advertising. The reply is stored in your account. The AI can make mistakes: check important answers. You can report any AI reply that is offensive, harmful or wrong with the Report button under it; the report (the reason, your note and the reply text) is sent to us so we can make the AI safer. Free accounts get a small number of AI messages each day; StudyGrit Pro gets more.

Authentication

We use Google Sign-In or email and password via Supabase. From Google we only receive your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) to identify your account. Signing in does not give StudyGrit access to your Google Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google data.

Cloud Storage

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS), encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Row Level Security means each account can only read and change its own notes, cards and files. Attached files sit in a private storage bucket and open only through short-lived links.

Usage Events

To learn which parts of StudyGrit help and which do not, the app and the website record simple usage events in your own account: for example that a note was saved, a search ran and how many results it found, or that a review session finished. When a search finds nothing, the words you searched are kept so we can make search better. Events also include timings (how long a step took) and error codes, so we can find and fix slow or broken parts (diagnostics). These events are never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Local Storage

The app and the website keep a small copy on your device so they open fast and keep a message you have not sent yet (for example your settings, a draft, and messages waiting for a connection). Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data removes that copy; your account data stays in the cloud until you delete your account.

AI Assistant Access (Optional)

You can make a private access token in Settings to let an AI assistant of your choice read and tidy your notes through the StudyGrit MCP server. Tokens are stored hashed, can be removed in Settings at any time, and are never shared with anyone else.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, StudyGrit sends the reminders you set (tasks, calendar blocks, revision, birthdays). They use standard web push; on Android they are delivered through Google's push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Only the reminder's title is included.

StudyGrit Voice (dictation app)

StudyGrit Voice turns your speech into text: the Android app (com.studygrit.voice) shows a floating microphone button over your other apps, and the Windows app does the same on a computer. It uses your StudyGrit account. This is exactly what it handles and why.

Microphone and audio

The app uses the microphone only while you dictate: from the moment you tap (or hold) the button until you stop. While it records, a notification and the red button show that it is listening. The recording is kept on your device only until our server confirms it has the text, then it is deleted (if an upload fails it is kept, so your words are not lost, and sent again later). Our server sends the audio over an encrypted connection to a third-party speech-recognition service that turns it into text and returns it. Audio is never stored on our servers and is never used for advertising; the service provider processes it only to give us the text.

Your dictated text

The text is tidied automatically (punctuation, filler words, the spellings in your personal dictionary) by a third-party text-processing service, then typed into the box you are using. Your dictation history (what you said and the tidied text), personal dictionary, corrections log and settings are stored in your private account so you can copy, edit or insert them again. History and the corrections log are deleted automatically after 60 days (and only your newest 200 dictations are kept); dictation records and usage events are deleted after 30 days.

Accessibility service (Android)

StudyGrit Voice uses the Android Accessibility service for one job: to type your dictated text into the text box you are using in another app, and to know when the keyboard is open so the button shows only while you type. To do this it looks at which box has focus, whether it is a password or number box (it never types into a password box), the text around your cursor at the moment it inserts your words (to put them in the right place and check they landed; this is not stored or sent), and the name of the app in front (so the button stays off in banking and payment apps, and so we can see which apps dictation works in). It does not collect or store what you type, what is on your screen, or your passwords, and it takes no action in other apps except inserting the text you dictated. You switch it on yourself in Android Settings after an in-app explanation and consent screen, and you can switch it off at any time.

Display over other apps (Android)

Used only to show the floating microphone button.

Voice usage events

The app records simple events in your account so we can make it faster and more reliable: for example that a dictation started, how long each step took, the name of the app the text went into, how it was inserted, and error codes. These events never contain your audio or your text.

Subscriptions

StudyGrit Voice Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive only the purchase token, the plan and its status, never your card or bank details. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app (Payments and subscriptions).

Sharing

We do not sell your data, the app shows no ads, and we share your audio and text with no one except the service providers described in this policy, only to provide dictation. Everything travels encrypted (TLS).

StudyGrit Pro (Subscriptions)

StudyGrit Pro (called Lifefile Pro in the Android app; monthly or yearly, and you can try it free for 14 days without a card) is bought in one of two ways. Inside the Android app it is bought through Google Play: Google handles the payment and we receive only the purchase token, the plan and its status. On the StudyGrit website it is bought through Gumroad, a payment processor: Gumroad handles the payment and receives your email address and your account id (so the plan reaches the right account), and we receive the plan, its status and Gumroad's purchase ids. We never see your card or bank details. A plan bought in Google Play is cancelled in the Google Play app (Payments and subscriptions); a plan bought on the website is cancelled from the link in your purchase receipt.

Data Deletion

You can delete any task, note or chat in the app or on the website, and any dictation in StudyGrit Voice History; it disappears from every device. To delete your whole account and everything stored with it, open the StudyGrit app, Settings, Delete account (or StudyGrit Voice, Settings, Delete account), or follow the steps at studygrit-website.studygrit-app.workers.dev/delete-account, or email us. Your voice data is deleted at once and the account itself within 30 days. Uninstalling an app removes the data on that device.

After your account is deleted, this is all that stays:

  • Free trial: if you used the free Pro trial, one-way codes (keyed hashes) made from the sign-in email you started it with and, if different, the one you had when you deleted the account. They stay while the trial offer runs, only so the trial is not given twice. They hold no email, account id or date, but we can tell whether a given email matches one.
  • Website purchases: Gumroad's sale and subscription ids, with the type, date and result of each payment event we handled, kept with no time limit so that no event is applied twice.
  • Deletion asked in StudyGrit Voice: your usage counters, plan record and device records stay until the sign-in account is deleted (within 30 days). The request itself stays with no time limit: your account id, email, dates, its status, whether you had Pro and how many items were deleted. The developer's Telegram alert of it holds your account id and a masked email.
  • Security and service records: StudyGrit Voice's daily count of app integrity checks with your account id (about 7 days), its sign-up step records with a random install id and no account id or email (about 90 days), and the server logs and job queues of our providers, which can hold your account id or email (up to 7 days).
  • Google sign-in: our sign-in service (Supabase) can keep records of a sign-in, including your account id and the tokens Google issued for it, until that service clears them.
  • Messages and alerts: emails you sent us, and the excerpt of any AI reply you reported that was sent to the developer on Telegram.
  • Held by others or required by law: records that Google Play, the payment processor and our other service providers keep under their own policies, and records the law requires us to keep (for example tax records of a payment), kept only as long as required.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Supabase (cloud database and file storage)
  • Cloudflare (the servers that run StudyGrit and StudyGrit Voice)
  • Our AI service provider (writes the AI chat's replies)
  • A third-party speech-recognition service (turns StudyGrit Voice audio into text)
  • A third-party text-processing service (tidies dictated text)
  • Google Play Billing (StudyGrit Pro and StudyGrit Voice Pro subscriptions)
  • Google's push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for notifications on Android
  • Gumroad (the payment processor for StudyGrit Pro bought on the website)
  • Telegram (the developer's own alert channel: when you report an AI reply, a short excerpt of it reaches the developer there - never who sent the report)

Each service has its own privacy policy.
